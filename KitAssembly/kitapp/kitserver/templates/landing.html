The Fundamentals

What is Gene Circuit Design?

Just like an electronic circuit controls the flow of electricity, a genetic circuit controls the flow of biological information within a cell.

By arranging biological components like promoters, repressors, and coding sequences, you can program living cells to sense environmental triggers, produce medicine, or change color in response to toxins.
